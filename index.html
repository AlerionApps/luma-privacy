Privacy Policy

Luma Planner  ·  Last updated: June 9, 2026

Luma Planner ("Luma", "we", "our", or "the app") is a personal productivity app built to help you plan your day, track habits, and stay focused. This policy explains what data is collected, why, and how it is handled. We are committed to keeping your information private and secure.

1. Data We Collect

Email Address Optional Not shared

If you choose to use the Google Drive Backup feature, we request your Google account email address via Google Sign-In. This is used solely to authenticate with your own Google Drive account so your backup file can be stored there. We do not store your email on any server we operate.

Backup Data Optional Your own Drive

When you manually trigger a backup, your local app database (tasks, habits, settings) is uploaded as a single encrypted file to your personal Google Drive — not to our servers. Only you have access to this file. You can delete it at any time from your Google Drive.

Purchase History Handled by Google Play

In-app purchases (Luma Pro subscriptions) are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your payment details. We receive only a confirmation of purchase status (active / inactive) which is stored locally on your device.

Advertising Data Shared with Google

Free users see ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (including the Android Advertising ID), app activity, and IP address to serve relevant ads. This data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalised ads in your device settings under Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation. Luma Pro subscribers see no ads.

2. Data Stored Locally (On Your Device Only)

All of your productivity data — tasks, events, deadlines, habit streaks, focus sessions, and app settings — is stored exclusively in a local database on your device. It never leaves your device unless you explicitly trigger a Google Drive backup.

3. Notifications

Luma sends local notifications for task reminders, event alerts, deadline warnings, and the daily midnight reset. These are scheduled entirely on-device. No notification content is transmitted to any server.

4. Notification Listener (Vacuum Focus Mode)

The Vacuum focus feature uses Android's Notification Listener permission to detect media playback controls from other apps (e.g. music apps) so they can be displayed in the focus dock. Luma does not read, store, log, or transmit the content of notifications from other apps. You can revoke this permission at any time in Settings → Notifications → Notification access.

5. Do Not Disturb Access

With your permission, Luma can toggle Do Not Disturb while you are in a Vacuum focus session. This is performed locally on your device and no information about your DND state is collected or transmitted.

6. Third-Party Services

The following third-party services are used in Luma. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy — covers Google Sign-In, Google Drive, Google Play Billing, and Google AdMob

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Your local data persists until you uninstall the app or clear its data from device settings. If you used Google Drive Backup, your backup file remains in your Google Drive until you delete it manually — Luma will never delete files from your Drive on your behalf. To remove all your data: uninstall the app and delete the luma_backup.db file from your Google Drive (if you created one).

8. Children's Privacy

Luma is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

9. Security

All network communication (Google Drive API, AdMob, Google Sign-In) uses HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit. Your local database is protected by Android's application sandboxing. No credentials or tokens are stored in plaintext.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
